# Whitespace

> Whitespace is an AI operating system for wholesale distributors (Y Combinator S26, London). It connects to a distributor's existing ERP and business systems and deploys self-improving AI agents that automate demand forecasting, inventory replenishment, order entry, quote generation, customer query responses, and accounts payable/receivable. As an early-stage company it ships a product but publishes no public API, developer documentation or SDK.

## Company
- [Whitespace website](https://whitespacehq.ai/): Product overview, customers, industries, platform, and FAQ.
- [Book a call](https://cal.com/atung/30min): Schedule a demo with the founders.

## Product
- Demand forecasting with seasonality and trend analysis.
- Inventory replenishment recommendations with lead-time optimization.
- Inventory analytics to identify slow-moving stock.
- AI agents for order entry, quote generation, customer query responses, accounts payable/receivable, procurement, and executive reporting.

## Integrations
- ERPs and business systems: SAP, NetSuite, Epicor, Infor, Microsoft Dynamics, Sage, Salesforce, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Gmail, Outlook.
